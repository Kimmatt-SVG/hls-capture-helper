Configure on-device storage and connect to your NAS over SMB.

Local video folder Downloads saved with Queue → Device

iOS stores downloads inside the app’s Documents folder. You can browse them in the Files app under On My iPhone → Movie Stream Downloader.

NAS connection (SMB) Direct SMB connection from the app

Unlike macOS Finder, iOS does not mount SMB shares system-wide. This app connects directly using your NAS credentials. You can also add the same share manually in Files → Browse → ⋯ → Connect to Server.
