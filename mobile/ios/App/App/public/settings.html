Settings

Configure on-device storage and connect to your NAS over SMB.

App download folder Videos saved with Queue → Device stay in this app’s storage

The app creates and uses its own Downloads folder inside iPhone storage. Browse it in the Files app under On My iPhone → Cinarip → Downloads. Other apps cannot use this folder unless you share files from Files.

NAS connection (SMB) Direct SMB connection from the app

Unlike macOS Finder, iOS does not mount SMB shares system-wide. This app connects directly using your NAS credentials. You can also add the same share manually in Files → Browse → ⋯ → Connect to Server.

Site login Opens Tornado so you can complete its captcha and sign in. The app keeps that session for downloads.
